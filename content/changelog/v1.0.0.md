The **first keystroke** 🎉 — **Keebtalogue** launches as a home for artisan keycap and keyboard enthusiasts to explore, document, and share artisan creations with the community.

### ✨ What's New

- Start your personal collection from makers, sculpts, and colorways.
- Build a wishlist to keep track of the caps you are hunting.
- Record sales and trades in your collection history.
- Find artisans with search and simple filters.
- Browse comfortably on any device with a mobile-friendly design.
