A **second wind** for **Keebtalogue** — a redesigned interface on a modern stack, link previews tailored to every page, and new ways to make the site your own.

### ✨ What's New

- A redesigned interface, rebuilt with **PrimeVue** (replacing Ant Design Vue).
- **Link previews** are now generated for every page, with dedicated designs for artisans and keycaps.
- Pin your favorite makers and sculpts.
- A global search, available on desktop as an early preview.
- Breadcrumbs and a left sidebar make it easier to find your way on desktop.
- A dedicated **Keycap Interest Checks** page.
- **Appearance settings** to choose your theme.
- A **feedback form** to share ideas and report problems.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Fixed pagination, page metadata, and data sync issues.
- Fixed mobile layout problems, including wishlist visibility and card layouts.
- Fixed broken links and missing data.

### 🚀 Improvements

- Search is faster and more accurate with full-text search.
- Pages are easier to find in search engines thanks to better meta tags.

### ⚠️ Breaking Changes

- The app moved to Nuxt 3.
