Welcome to the **keyboard era** — Keebdex goes beyond keysets and artisans with full keyboard support, richer collection tracking, and a stronger foundation for everything ahead.

### ✨ What's New

- A new **Keyboards** module with **brands**, **keyboards**, **releases**, and **variants**, including design lineage and standardized specs.
- **Keyboard collections**: track the boards you own alongside your keysets and artisans.
- A **User Management** page for admins to manage access and roles.
- The keyset form suggests existing designers as you type, while still accepting new names.
- The sculpt form gains collection editing and revision suggestions.
- A redesigned **Keebdex logo** with a new Dosis wordmark.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Opening a sculpt page from a colorway link now jumps to the right page and opens the colorway card, which closes cleanly.
- The keyset form now starts with the right default values when creating or editing.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **Keyset pages** have an archivist-inspired design with a thumbnail strip to browse kits.
- **Sharing a collection** is simpler: choose private or public, and _Copy Link_ publishes it for you when needed.
- **Search** now finds partial or slightly misspelled terms, so "gundam" finds "GUNDAMHAKU".
- Maker, brand, sculpt, and keyboard pages keep their details in a drawer for a cleaner header.
- Logo cards look the same across every module, in an equal-height grid.
- The **Wishlist Image** builder offers configurable column layouts with a preview sized to its content.
- Stricter data validation keeps records accurate.

### ⚠️ Breaking Changes

- **Keycaps** are now **Keysets** everywhere, including page addresses.
