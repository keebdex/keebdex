Throw the **doors open** — anyone signed in can now contribute to the catalog. This release adds community submissions for keyboards, keysets, and artisan colorways with full moderation behind them, plus theme presets and a trading-card colorway preview.

### ✨ What's New

- **Community submissions**: propose artisan colorways, keyset kits, and keyboard variants (or a new sculpt, keyset, or keyboard) through a step-by-step wizard. Submissions stay _Pending_ until reviewed.
- _Submit a Colorway/Kit/Release/Variant_ links on detail pages open the wizard at the right step.
- **Review pages** for artisans, keysets, and keyboards let moderators approve or reject submissions one by one or in bulk, and let submitters edit or delete their own.
- Contributions from staff assigned to the brand, profile, or maker go live right away, while everyone else's wait in the review queue.
- The colorway preview becomes a Yu-Gi-Oh!- or Pokémon-style **trading card**.
- **Theme presets** (_Analog Dreams_, _Aurora_, _Carbon_, _EVA-01_, _Parchment_, _Taro_) switch instantly, and your theme and color mode sync across devices.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Saving now waits for images to finish uploading, and form errors say what went wrong instead of "Something went wrong".
- Renaming a keyboard updates its address, and adding a sculpt from the sculpt form works again.
- Artisan statistics now count each day correctly instead of being off by one day.
- Keyset order graphs and history previews open again, and logos invert correctly in the profile drawer.
- Management pages no longer scroll sideways, and buttons keep their labels on phones.
- Items marked as sold hide their asking price, and want-to-buy or trade items require contact info.

### 🚀 Improvements

- Site notices and cookie consent show as toasts until you acknowledge them, replacing the top banner.
- Editors and makers can rename sculpts, and the sculpt address follows the new name.
- Keyboards record top case and mounting styles, with several choices allowed.
- Images are served from a CDN, so pages load faster wherever you are.
