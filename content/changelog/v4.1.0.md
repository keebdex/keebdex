Light up the sky with **Aurora Borealis** — a refreshed teal identity with its own brand guidelines, a page for community shoutouts, and smarter tools for managing colors.

### ✨ What's New

- A **Brand** page (`/brand`) with logo variants, the favicon mark, the color palette, the type scale, and usage do's and don'ts.
- A new **Aurora** look: the accent color moves from rose to teal across the wordmark, the app icon, and the whole interface.
- **Import colors from CSV** with a preview, and filter colors by system.
- **RAL codes** fill in their hex value and name automatically, with a refetch action for supported systems.
- A **Shoutouts** page (`/shoutouts`) to browse stories and feedback from the community.
- Admins can review and resolve feedback and turn it into featured shoutouts.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Duplicate colors can no longer be created, and malformed CSV files no longer break the import.

### 🚀 Improvements

- Adding a color suggests codes, warns about likely duplicates, and explains the display name.
- Search fields across forms behave the same way, with consistent minimum lengths, filters, and paging.
