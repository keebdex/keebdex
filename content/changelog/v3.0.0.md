A **new horizon** — Keebtalogue becomes **Keebdex**, fully responsive on a modern stack, with refreshed pages and new tools to organize your collections.

### ✨ What's New

- **Keebtalogue is now Keebdex**, rebuilt on Nuxt 4 and Nuxt UI 4 for a faster, more polished experience.
- Every page now adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops.
- Refreshed **About** and **Privacy** pages.
- New **Changelog** and **Why Choose Keebdex?** pages.
- **Custom sorting** for collections, with saved preferences, a dedicated sorting page, and a live preview.
- A **Priority** toggle highlights your most important items in collection and wishlist previews.
- **Keycap Colors**: color swatches on keycap sets, with pages to manage them.
- Browse keycaps by **status** or by **profile and manufacturer** on dedicated pages.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Fixed stability issues found during the move to the new stack.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **Search** lives in the command palette and covers artisans, keycap sets, and more, with results grouped by maker.
- Pages respond and render faster.
- Admin and moderator messages read the same way across the app.

### ⚠️ Breaking Changes

- The move to Nuxt 4 and Nuxt UI 4 may affect custom integrations.
