**Chroma craft** for the artisan community — submit and manage colorways through a sculpt-focused workflow, price your listings, and enjoy a snappier site.

### ✨ What's New

- **Submit and manage artisan colorways** through a workflow built around each sculpt.
- Add **asking prices** to items in your selling collections.
- A site-wide **banner** for important announcements.
- Keycap kits get a **customizable display name**.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Colorway submissions and cards now always include the sculpt name.
- Collections reload correctly once your session has loaded.
- Keycap updates save to the right set.
- Marketplace keycaps are sorted by status by default again.

### 🚀 Improvements

- The collection item form is simpler, with clearer hints.
- Every action shows a consistent success or error message.
- Shared links show a refreshed preview image with better typography and layout.
- Faster, more reliable deployments.
