Reviewing submissions gets a sharper eye — every submitted image now shows its original size, so blurry or tiny photos stand out before they are approved.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **Image sizes in submissions**: the review queues have a _Size_ column with each image's original width and height (file size and format on hover), and the submission forms show the size under every image field. Images smaller than 512 pixels on their longer side are highlighted as low resolution, so moderators can spot them quickly and submitters are prompted to upload a sharper photo.
