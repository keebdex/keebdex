title: Unreleased
---

**Catalog pages get the archive treatment** — every keyboard now reads like a catalog entry, with a large photo beside its design and its releases and variants on one timeline, and sculpt pages show their full record and every colorway's details.

### ✨ What's New

- **A new home page** shows the newest colorways as a wall of keycaps you can open, how big each catalog has grown, and a search bar right under the headline. _Happening Now_ lists today's busiest makers and the group buys closing soonest with how much time is left, and the page ends with a way to submit a colorway, keyset, or keyboard.
- **Keyboard pages** open as a catalog entry: a large photo you can switch between variants from the strip below it, beside the keyboard's name, story, and design specs.
- **Production History** lays every release out on a timeline with its story, specs, and its variants as photo cards, so you can follow how a keyboard evolved and open any variant for its full details.
- **Sculpt pages** open with the sculpt's details: its maker, profile, cast, design, release, collection, and story, with a link to the sculpt it revises. On phones the details fold away under _Sculpt Details_, so the colorways are right there. Opening a colorway slides in a panel with its photo and every detail, from its release and units to its stem, ready to save or turn into a share card.
- **Derived Designs** sit right under a keyboard's details, so the keyboards built on its design are easy to find even when it has many releases, and a keyboard based on another links to its original next to its name.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Links you share from Keebdex now preview the page you shared, with its own title and description, instead of the home page's, and search results show the full keyset name, such as _GMK CYL Olivia_.
- An address that leads nowhere, such as a renamed keyset or a maker that was never added, now shows a proper _Not Found_ page with a way back home, instead of an empty page or an error.
- A keyboard variant now shows its full name, both when opened and in the copied image, and _Save_ there adds that variant to your collection instead of the wrong item.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **The home page** moves more smoothly: the welcome eases in, the keycap wall drops into place, and each section fades up as you scroll to it, while _Reduce motion_ in your system settings keeps everything still.
- **The _Wishlist Image_** has a cleaner look: each cap shows its sculpt and maker, with the price, _Top Priority_, and _Found_ or _Sold_ marked right on the photo, and your terms, like _Shipping Included_, sit right beside the caps for sale. Its settings are grouped so it is easier to pick a list type, its collections, and your contacts.
- **The _Trading Hub_** shows each wishlist and sale as a card with a page of its caps, so you can see what collectors want or offer at a glance, and _How to List Your Caps_ in the toolbar walks you through getting your own caps listed.
- **Variant details** slide in from any variant with its front and back photos and everything about it, from the keyboard's design to the variant's own materials, ready to save or copy as an image. A release can also name the top case layouts it was sold in, and otherwise shows the keyboard's, and empty specs no longer leave blank labels behind.
- **Catalog lists** match the new catalog pages. Makers and brands open with a clear header and your pinned makers first, profiles are grouped under the company that makes them, so GMK's CYL and MTNU sit on one card, and maker, brand, and profile pages show the logo, story, and links up front instead of behind an _Info_ button. Group buy cards show how many days are left, _My Collection_ marks each shelf's type and whether it is public, and the caps, keysets, and keyboards in a collection get the same cards as catalog pages, linking back to their pages.
- **Colorway search** results use the same cards as sculpt pages, and _Save_ is labeled the same way everywhere, on phones too.
- **Keyset pages** list the designer, row sculpt, dates, and status beside the cover in the same rows as keyboard and sculpt pages, so every catalog page reads the same way. _Save_, _Edit_, and _History_ sit under the keyset's name, the Geekhack thread and order charts are labeled buttons under the specs, and kits and colors are added, edited, and removed right on the page instead of on separate management pages.
