**Many hands** keep the catalog right — anyone signed in can now suggest a fix, a missing photo, or a keyset's colors on any published page, and propose a maker, brand, or keycap profile the catalog is missing along with their submission. Moderators review every change before it goes live, with the full history of each page and one-click undo, and the new _Leaderboard_ celebrates the collectors who add the most.

### ✨ What's New

- **Edit for everyone**: signed-in collectors can fix or fill in details on any published page with the same _Edit_ button editors use, from a colorway or kit up to a maker, brand, or keycap profile, including the colors a keyset is missing or should drop, and records without a picture show _Add Photo_ or _Add Cover_. Moderators review suggestions in the new _Edits_ tab of the Submissions pages, apply all or only some of the changes, and see when a detail changed since it was suggested or when someone else suggested another value, with a count on each _Submissions_ link instead of a notification for every new one.
- **Propose a new maker, brand, or profile**: when the submission forms don't list the maker, keyboard brand, or keycap profile you need, propose it right there with your sculpt, keyboard, or keyset, and pick a new keyset's colors as you add it. Moderators review it with the rest of your submission, and it joins the catalog once approved.
- **Change history**: staff can open a _History_ panel on maker, sculpt, profile, keyset, brand, and keyboard pages, or on a single colorway, kit, release, or variant, to see every change, who made it, and what it looked like before, including the colors added to or removed from a keyset. They can undo an edit or restore an earlier version, even one with a photo that was replaced long ago, and the new _Recent Changes_ page shows what changed across every page they manage.
- **Leaderboard**: the new _Leaderboard_ page ranks the collectors who add the most to Keebdex, all time or this month and for each module, with points for every approved submission and accepted edit. The home page shows this month's top three on a podium, and you can hide yourself from the board in your account settings and still see your own rank.
- **A colorway card to share**: tapping a colorway now opens a clean card in your site theme with just its photo and name beside the maker's logo, ready to copy or download and share. Pick _Keebdex Detailed_ to add the release, sale, price, and quantity, or one of the Yu-Gi-Oh! and Pokémon trading cards.
- **Stem types for colorways**: colorway forms let you pick one or more stem types, such as MX, Alps, or Topre, so you can tell at a glance whether a cap fits your board.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Colorways, keysets, keyboards, releases, and variants submitted by the community no longer appear on catalog pages, in counts, in search, or in the home page lists until a moderator approves them. Keysets added directly by staff no longer go missing from profile and Group Buy lists.
- On computers, pages no longer run past the right edge of the window, which cut off the end of toolbars and the last column of grids and pushed the home page off center.
- Link previews on Discord and other sites show the Keebdex font again, instead of a plain fallback font that only appeared once a page was shared.
- Keyset covers and kit pictures that link to images hosted by a vendor's own image service show again, instead of a blank space, while waiting to be copied to Keebdex, which now copies them too.
- The _Unread_ filter on the notifications page loads your unread notifications directly, so older unread ones show up even when many newer ones are already read.
- Profile pictures from Google accounts show again on pages with many collectors, such as shoutouts and change history, instead of falling back to a placeholder.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **Images in submissions**: forms show the current or just-uploaded image with _Replace_ and _Remove_ buttons instead of an empty upload box, and forms and review queues show each image's original size and flag low-resolution photos, so they are easy to spot and reject with a ready-made note.
- **Combined quick replies**: moderators can pick several quick replies when rejecting a submission or resolving feedback, and they appear as a tidy list in the note.
- **Renames that move the page**: renaming a sculpt, keyset, or keyboard, by staff or through a suggested edit, gives the page an address that matches its new name, and its colorways, kits, or releases come along. Its change history, pending suggestions, and editors follow it too, and sculpts that sync from a maker's Google Doc or website keep syncing instead of coming back under their old name.
- **A livelier home page**: the welcome leads straight in with _Start Collecting_ and _Search Keebdex_, and on phones it takes less room so the latest releases show without scrolling. _Happening Now_ shows how many days each live group buy has left, short lists sit still instead of scrolling past as repeats, and shoutouts put the quote first with its author below.
- **Group buys that keep up**: the _Live_ tab of _Group Buys_ and the home page's _Happening Now_ show every set whose group buy is running today, going by its dates, so a set appears and leaves on time without anyone updating it. _Group Buys_ now has just _Interest Check_ and _Live_, a keyset's status only says whether it is an interest check, confirmed, or cancelled, and the row sculpt is now labelled _Row Sculpt_.
- **Order graphs that last**: order graphs and order history charts on keyset pages are now stored on Keebdex like keyset covers, so they keep showing after the image host they were shared on removes them.
- **Account menu on phones**: the account menu now shows your role at the top on phones, where the account button is just your picture, and the _Sign In_ button keeps its label instead of showing only an icon.

### ⚠️ Breaking Changes

- The _Ended_ tab of _Group Buys_ is gone, and links to it open the _Live_ tab. Finished group buys are still listed on each profile's page.
- Renaming a sculpt, keyset, or keyboard now changes its address, and the old address no longer opens the page. Bookmarks or shared links to a renamed page need to be updated.
