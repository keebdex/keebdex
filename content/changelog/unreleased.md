### ✨ What's New

- **The Supporter badge** is now separate from staff roles, so editors, makers, and designers who donate keep their access and show both, such as _Editor · Supporter_ in the profile menu, where supporters also get a glowing ring and a heart on their avatar. Getting the badge sends its own thank-you notification, and staff notifications now say which role you have and what it lets you manage. Admins turn it on with a _Supporter_ switch in _User Management_, where supporters also have their own filter.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **Adding a Pantone color** fills in its hex value and name from Pantone's color finder as soon as you type the code, such as _185 C_ or _19-4052 TCX_, the same way RAL codes already do, so there is no need to pick the color from Pantone's site by hand. Since Pantone does not publish hex values, Pantone swatches note that theirs are approximate and for reference only.
- **Change history names each sync after the bot that made it**, such as _Bowerbird_ for the artisan sync or _Pelican_ for images, and hovering the name shows where the data came from, such as _Google Docs_ or _Alpha Keycaps Website_, instead of a generic _Maker Sites_. New sync jobs and sources show up there on their own.
- **Searching with symbols** such as _&_, _(_, a comma, or a quote now finds what you typed instead of failing or returning nothing, so a name like _Jelly & Jam_ is easy to look up across the catalog, the keyset designer and sculpt pickers, colors, and colorway search.
