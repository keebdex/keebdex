**Keyboards get the archive treatment** — every keyboard now reads like a catalog entry, with a large photo beside its full record, and its releases and variants on one timeline.

### ✨ What's New

- **Keyboard pages** open as a catalog entry: a large photo you can switch between variants from the strip below it, beside the keyboard's design and a record of what Keebdex knows about it, from the years it was produced and how many releases, variants, and units it has, to its sale formats, finishes, and when it was last verified.
- **Production History** lays every release out on a timeline, named with its year, alongside its story, specs, and its variants as photo cards, so you can follow how a keyboard evolved and open any variant for its full details.
- **Lineage** shows the design a keyboard is based on and every keyboard derived from it right on the page.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- A keyboard variant now shows its full name, both when opened and in the copied image, and _Save_ there adds that variant to your collection instead of the wrong item.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **Variant details** slide in from any variant with its front and back photos and everything about it, from the keyboard's design to the variant's own materials, ready to save or copy as an image. A release can also name the top case layouts it was sold in, and empty specs no longer leave blank labels behind.
- **Keyset pages** gather the designer, row sculpt, dates, and status into one _Specifications_ grid with icons, in the same style as keyboard pages. _Save_, _Edit_, and _History_ sit under the keyset's name, the Geekhack thread and order charts are labeled buttons under the specs, and kits and colors are added, edited, and removed right on the page instead of on separate management pages.
