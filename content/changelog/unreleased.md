**Catalog pages get the archive treatment** — a new home page shows what is happening across the dex, every keyboard reads like a catalog entry with its releases on one timeline, and sculpt pages show their full record and every colorway's details.

### ✨ What's New

- **A new home page** shows the newest colorways as a wall of keycaps, how big each catalog has grown, and a search bar under the headline, while _Happening Now_ lists today's busiest makers and the group buys closing soonest. It eases in as you scroll, and _Reduce motion_ in your system settings keeps it still.
- **Keyboard pages** open with a large photo, switchable between variants, beside the keyboard's name, story, and design specs, followed by its derived designs and a link to the original it is based on. _Production History_ lays every release out on a timeline, and each variant slides in with its front and back photos and full specs, ready to save or copy as an image.
- **Sculpt pages** open with the sculpt's maker, profile, cast, design, release, and story. Opening a colorway slides in a panel with its photo and every detail, ready to save or turn into a share card.
- **Color Swatches** lay every keycap color out as a wall you can filter by matching system or search by code or name, and each color shows its hex and RGB values and the keysets that use it. A keyset's _Color Palette_ uses the same swatches, so you can jump to other sets that share a color.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Shared links preview the page you shared instead of the home page, and search results show full keyset names, such as _GMK CYL Olivia_.
- An address that leads nowhere, such as a renamed keyset, shows a _Not Found_ page with a way back home instead of an empty page or an error.
- A keyboard variant shows its full name when opened and in copied images, _Save_ adds the right variant, and variants sold as a giveaway, commission, auction, or friends and family show their own price instead of their release's.
- Opening a link to one of your private collections, or reloading its page, shows the collection instead of an empty page.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **Catalog lists** match the new pages: profiles are grouped under their maker, owner pages show the logo, story, and links up front, group buys show the days left, and keyset pages list the designer, dates, and status beside the cover with the Geekhack thread and order charts under the specs.
- **Navigation** is simpler: the top bar says where you are while the page names itself once below it, phones get a back button to the page above, and the sidebar keeps only Leaderboard, Notifications, Feedback, and Donate at the bottom. Your profile menu holds the rest, including _Appearance_ with its _Theme_ and _Palette_ choices.
- **Wide screens** keep every page's content at a comfortable width in the middle, so grids, tables, and forms no longer stretch across an ultrawide monitor.
- **The _Changelog_** has a _Releases_ index, beside the notes on computers and folded into a bar on phones, that follows where you are and jumps to any release, with a link you can share.
- **Pins** have no limit, work for keyboard brands and keyboards too, and sit in their own _Pinned_ group above the rest.
- **_My Collection_** previews each shelf's first photos and shows whether it is public, a wishlist, or for sale, and items inside use the same cards as catalog pages.
- **The _Wishlist Image_** marks the price, _Top Priority_, and _Found_ or _Sold_ on each cap, with your terms beside the caps for sale, and your wishlist and for-sale collections show the same marks. The _Trading Hub_ shows each list as a card of its caps with a guide to listing your own.
- **_Recent Changes_** lets staff step through each edit beside the list, compare fields side by side or one under the other, and undo or restore it without leaving the page, and staff can manage a keyset's kits and colors right on its page.
