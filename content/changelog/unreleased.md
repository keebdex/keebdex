title: Unreleased
---

**Catalog pages get the archive treatment** — every keyboard now reads like a catalog entry, with a large photo beside its design and its releases and variants on one timeline, and sculpt pages show their full record and every colorway's details.

### ✨ What's New

- **A new home page** shows the newest colorways as a wall of keycaps, how big each catalog has grown, and a search bar under the headline. _Happening Now_ lists today's busiest makers and the group buys closing soonest.
- **Keyboard pages** open as a catalog entry: a large photo, switchable between variants, beside the keyboard's name, story, and design specs.
- **Production History** lays every release out on a timeline with its story, specs, and variants, so you can follow how a keyboard evolved.
- **Sculpt pages** open with the sculpt's maker, profile, cast, design, release, and story, folded under _Sculpt Details_ on phones. Opening a colorway slides in a panel with its photo and every detail, ready to save or turn into a share card.
- **Color Swatches** lay every keycap color out as a wall of swatches, with a tab per matching system and a search by code or name. Opening one shows its hex and RGB values, ready to copy, and the keysets that use it. A keyset's _Color Palette_ uses the same swatches, so you can jump from any of its colors to the other sets that share it.
- **Derived Designs** sit right under a keyboard's details, and a keyboard based on another links to its original next to its name.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Links you share from Keebdex now preview the page you shared instead of the home page, and search results show full keyset names, such as _GMK CYL Olivia_.
- An address that leads nowhere, such as a renamed keyset, now shows a _Not Found_ page with a way back home instead of an empty page or an error.
- A keyboard variant shows its full name, both when opened and in the copied image, and _Save_ there adds the right variant to your collection. Variants sold as a giveaway, commission, auction, or friends and family no longer show their release's price, only one of their own.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **The home page** eases in as you scroll, and _Reduce motion_ in your system settings keeps it still.
- **The _Wishlist Image_** marks the price, _Top Priority_, and _Found_ or _Sold_ right on each cap, with your terms beside the caps for sale, and its settings are grouped so lists, collections, and contacts are easier to pick. On wide screens the settings stay open beside your image, and on phones the gear button opens them.
- **The _Trading Hub_** shows each wishlist and sale as a card of its caps, and _How to List Your Caps_ walks you through listing your own.
- **Variant details** slide in from any variant with its front and back photos and full specs, ready to save or copy as an image, and empty specs no longer leave blank labels.
- **Catalog lists** match the new catalog pages: profiles are grouped under their maker, so GMK's CYL and MTNU share one card, owner pages show the logo, story, and links up front, group buys show the days left, and colorway search uses the same cards as sculpt pages.
- **Pins** have no limit anymore and now work for keyboard brands and keyboards too, and whatever you pin sits in a _Pinned_ group of its own above the rest, so it is easy to tell apart.
- **_My Collection_** gives each shelf a peek at its first photos and shows whether it is public and whether it is a wishlist or for sale. Items inside a collection use the same cards as catalog pages, and _Save_ and _Move_ keep their labels on phones too.
- **_Recent Changes_** lists changes beside the change you are reviewing, so staff can step through each edit, compare the fields side by side or one under the other, and undo or restore it without leaving the page. Page histories show changed fields the same way, marking each one as added, modified, or removed.
- **Keyset pages** list the designer, row sculpt, dates, and status beside the cover like other catalog pages, link the Geekhack thread and order charts under the specs, and let staff manage kits and colors right on the page.
