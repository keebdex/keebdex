**Keyboard pages get lighter** — each keyboard now opens with its key specs at a glance, and empty details no longer leave blank labels behind.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- A keyboard variant now shows its full name, both when opened and in the copied image, and _Save_ there adds that variant to your collection instead of the wrong item.

### 🚀 Improvements

- **Keyboard pages** open with the brand logo, the description, and the form factor, top case, mount, and typing angle once under the title, with a _Based on_ link to the original design when there is one. Releases list only the specs they have, and variant cards show their finish and price. Opening a variant slides in a panel with its front and back photos and everything about it, from the keyboard's design to the variant's own materials, ready to save or copy as an image. A release can also name the top case layouts it was sold in.
- **Keyset pages** gather the designer, row sculpt, dates, and status into one _Specifications_ grid with icons, in the same style as keyboard pages. _Save_, _Edit_, and _History_ sit under the keyset's name, the Geekhack thread and order charts are labeled buttons under the specs, and kits and colors are added, edited, and removed right on the page instead of on separate management pages.
