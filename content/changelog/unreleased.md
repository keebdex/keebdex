**Boost the signal** — Keebdex now tells you when your submissions are reviewed, your feedback is resolved, or there is news worth knowing. This release also brings the wishlist builder to your phone, turns keysets into a proper catalog, and makes every grid load faster.

### ✨ What's New

- **Notifications** tell you when your submissions are approved, rejected, or deleted (with the moderator's note), when your feedback is resolved, and when your role changes. Choose what you hear about in **Notification settings**.
- **Announcements** bring Keebdex news to their own tab under Notifications, with the top ones shown as toasts; dismiss one and it stays gone on every device.
- The **Wishlist Image** builder works on phones, and a new **Save & Share** window copies, downloads, or shares the image, or copies your trading list as Discord/Reddit-ready text.
- A **colorway search page**, opened from _View all_ in search, updates as you type, matches exact terms, groups by maker, and saves to your collections.
- **Keysets** open on a catalog of manufacturers grouped by profile, with group buys on one _Group Buys_ page (Interest Check / Live / Ended).
- **Keyset pages** read like a catalog entry: a fixed cover, set details apart from group buy history, a full-width palette, and a grid of kits with price and unit count.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Keyset pages without kits still show the set's details, profile pages no longer show _Not Found_ when opened directly, and paging past the end no longer errors.
- Visitors to a shared collection no longer see the owner's actions, and _My Collection_ caps no longer look sold.
- Wishlist cards can no longer be dragged between the Want and Have lists, where they could not be removed.
- Colorway cards on narrow phones keep their photo inside the card, and extra action buttons wrap onto a second line instead of stretching it.
- Sidebar items no longer show another item's icon and color after loading the page signed in.

### 🚀 Improvements

- Grids load images sized for your screen, so pages load faster, especially on phones.
- **Colorway cards** get a holographic foil that sweeps across and then rests; hover to hide it.
- The _Save_ button is available to every signed-in user, with a _New Collection_ option to start a collection right from the item.
- The **collection form** explains each kind of collection and how public wishlists and for-sale lists appear in the **Trading Hub**.
- Copied and downloaded images are the same size on every device, sharper, and copy in Safari too.
- Status colors match each theme, dark logos invert reliably, and empty pages are friendlier.
- The search palette lists artisan colorways in a single list, closest name matches first, and matches every word you type.
- **About** and **Changelog** moved to the profile menu, and signed-in feedback no longer asks for your name and email.
