Sharpen your **collector's edge** — this release focuses on collection management and wishlists, so you can curate with precision and style.

### ✨ What's New

- Mark items as _Found_ or _Sold_ to track where your collection stands.
- Manage artisan keycaps alongside your other collectibles.
- Wishlist images look more polished.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Editors and makers can now edit exactly the pages assigned to them.
- Corrected outdated artisan data and improved the guidance around it.
- Fixed a few minor stability issues.

### 🚀 Improvements

- Collection handling is smoother and more reliable.
- Managing collections now requires signing in, keeping your data private.
