# Artisan Forms and Wishlist

Feature reference for Keebdex agents; project-wide rules live in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

Artisan rules that are not part of submissions or edit suggestions: sculpt renames, the colorway stem field, and the Wishlist Image builder.

- **Sculpt renames**: Renaming a sculpt (`app/components/artisan/modal/SculptForm.vue`) always changes its slug, through any path (the form, an approved edit suggestion, a revert). Colorways reference sculpts by `maker_sculpt_id` (on update cascade), and `update_maker_sculpt_id()` takes a cascaded key's maker and slug on colorways, so they follow the sculpt in the same statement; a rename writes one sculpt revision and none for the colorways (see `docs/agents/revision-history.md`). catalog-sync matches sculpts by slug, so `keep_source_slug()` saves the old slug of a `gdoc`/`scraper` sculpt to `source_slug` on its first rename and the sync matches on `coalesce(source_slug, sculpt_id)`; colorway images keep their old path (`update_maker_sculpt_id()` in `20260101000700_baseline_triggers`, the column and `keep_source_slug()` in `20261021000100_page_renames`, the protected fields in `20261017000000_edit_suggestions`). A suggested rename suggests the new `sculpt_id` too (`slugKeys`, see `docs/agents/edit-suggestions.md`); `maker_sculpt_id` and `source_slug` stay protected. The sculpt page follows a revert that changes the slug (`reverted` carries the written fields).
- **Colorway stem**: _Stem_ in `app/components/artisan/modal/ColorwayForm.vue` is a multi-select over `artisanStems` (`app/utils/schemas/artisan.ts`: MX, TMX, Alps, Topre, Choc; `stem` is plain `text[]`, not an enum), plus any saved value outside that list so it can be removed; no custom values. An empty selection is saved as `null`. `stem` is one of the Google Doc-managed fields, so editing it adds it to `overridden_fields` (fields are compared with `isEqual`, so arrays count only when their values change).
- **Wishlist Image builder** (`/artisan/wishlist`, all devices; `app/components/artisan/wishlist/`): `ArtisanWishlistPreview` shows an editable `ArtisanWishlistSheet` (two compact columns on phones, drag after a 200 ms press on touch) and captures a second, static sheet through `SharedSnapshot` at `wishlistColumns`/`wishlistGridWidth` (`app/utils/wishlist.ts`). A sheet opens with the wordmark beside the contacts (Discord, Reddit, QQ as transparent badges in their brand colors), then one section per list with items (Want, then Have on a trading list): a heading with a colored bar (info for Want, warning for Have or a selling list) and, beside the sections for sale, the seller's terms when set (_Shipping Included_, _PayPal F&F Only, No Buyer Protection_), over an `ArtisanWishlistGrid` (each its own drag group) of `ArtisanWishlistItem`s. The footer holds the date and `AppWordmark domain`. In the editor the sheet is framed as a page. An item is the square photo with the colorway name and its sculpt and maker under it (`GET /api/users/:id/collection-items` embeds both names); over the photo sit a small price tag (_Offer_ or _Ask_), a _Top Priority_ badge (only the star on phones), a _Found_ or _Sold_ stamp on a greyed photo when it is no longer listed, and in the editor the Top Priority and Remove buttons, which do not start a drag. _Save & Share_ opens `ArtisanWishlistShareModal`: an Image tab (renders on open; Copy Image and Download, plus Share Image on phones with Web Share) and a Text tab (`wishlistText`, Markdown or plain, format in the `wishlist-text-format` cookie). Settings are a `USidebar` (slideover below `lg`): Buying / Selling / Trading tabs (text only, since tab icons hide the labels in the narrow sidebar) (switching resets the collections, titles, and terms), then Want (or List) and Have groups with a collection and a title, Layout (columns), Contact, and the seller Terms as switches. From `lg` up the sidebar always sits beside the sheet, with no Hide/Show toggle; below `lg` it is a slideover, closed at first, that the navbar's gear button opens (shown once a collection is picked). Its header (the `header` slot) is an `h2` _Wishlist Settings_, padded like the preview's navbar, so themes give it the same heading font as the navbar's `h1` (the sidebar's `title` prop renders a plain `p`), and a close button shown only below `lg`. `useWishlistSidebar()` stays `true`: `USidebar` keeps the slideover's state apart and restores the open sidebar when the window widens, even after it was closed at a narrow width. Until a collection is picked, `ArtisanWishlistStart` stands in for the sheet: a card with the list type tabs and the collection picker (or a link to My Collection when no artisan collection fits the type) over a faded skeleton of the image, so the settings button appears only once a collection is picked. The type tabs and collection filters come from `useWishlistPicker()` (`app/composables/useTradingConfig.ts`, with `WISHLIST_TYPE_ITEMS`), shared with the settings: changing the type resets the config to its defaults, keeping the contacts and columns. The drag tip is dismissed through the `wishlist-drag-hint-seen` cookie.
- **Share card**: the cards in `ArtisanColorwayCard`'s popup are the theme-styled `ArtisanColorwayShareCard`, as wide as the 380×550px trading cards: _Keebdex Simple_ by default, only the photo and a one-line `colorwayTitle`, or _Keebdex Detailed_, 550px tall with the photo taking what the text leaves; the maker's logo is the avatar of the title and badges, and it signs off on the right with `AppWordmark domain`, which spreads `org` under _dex_ after the bar. Being fixed in size, they are captured in place rather than through `SharedSnapshot`.
- **Colorway search** (`server/utils/colorway-search.ts`): matches published colorways (`PUBLISHED_FILTER`) by their own name only (every word of the term, `deleted` excluded); maker/sculpt-only matches are left to their own palette groups. `GET /api/search` (the `KeebSearch` palette) returns the 24 most relevant colorways (`searchPaletteColorways`: exact → prefix → phrase) with `total` and `viewAllTo`, and the palette appends a "View all N artisan colorways" row when `total` exceeds the shown items. `GET /api/search/colorways?q=&page=&size=&exact=` backs the `/artisan/search` results page, ordered by maker then sculpt (`size` capped at 72), returning `{ data, total }` and an empty page with the real total when `page` is past the end; `exact=1` keeps names containing the whole term as separate words (Postgres `imatch` with `\m`/`\M`, applied through `.filter()` because the client has no `imatch` helper). The page searches as the user types (400 ms debounce, `router.replace`), keeps `q`, `page`, `exact=1`, and `group=maker` in the URL, groups the current page by maker client-side, and offers Save to Collection for signed-in users. Other palette groups stay palette-only.
