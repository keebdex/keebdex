# Collections

Feature reference for Keebdex agents; project-wide rules live in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

User collections, the Save/Move menu every module uses, and the Trading Hub.

- **Collections** (`app/pages/collection/`, `app/components/collection/modal/`): `user_collections.intent` is shown to users as **My Collection** (`keep`), **Wishlist** (`want`), and **For Sale or Trade** (`sell`) through `collectionIntentItems` (`app/utils/collections.ts`); never show the word "intent". `CollectionForm.vue` validates with `collectionSchema` (`app/utils/schemas/collection.ts`, Discord contact required unless `keep`) and never sends `published`, which only the Share modal changes. A published `want`/`sell` artisan collection is listed in the Trading Hub (`/artisan/marketplace`, `GET /api/marketplace?page=&size=&intent=`), and the Share modal says so. The endpoint pages collections with at least one listed (`exchange`) item, newest first (`size` default 20, capped at 50; an inner join on the items keeps empty collections out of pages and the total; `intent=want|sell` narrows to one kind), and returns `{ data, total }` with each collection's listed `items` (colorway with sculpt and maker names). The page keeps `intent` (All / Buying / Selling toolbar tabs, switching resets the page) and `page` (`usePagination(20)`) in the URL, and keeps the three steps to get listed (_Create a Collection_, _Add Your Caps_, _Make It Public_) behind _How to List Your Caps_ on the right of the toolbar (icon only on phones): a `UPopover` holding a vertical `UStepper` (default size with a smaller `size-8` circle), a guide rather than a wizard, so it is `disabled` with every step shown done (`default-value` on the last). `ArtisanMarketplaceListing` lays the listings out as a `UPageGrid` of `ArtisanTradeCard`s: a binder page of the first six caps (three columns, empty cells for fewer, the last cell covered by a `+N` count of the rest), the collection name with a Buying/Selling badge, its message, Discord contact, cap count, and date. It shows card skeletons while loading and `UEmpty` when empty; its slideover shows offer/asking prices and Top Priority badges, links each cap to its sculpt page (`?cid=`), and offers View Collection and Copy Discord (only when a contact is set). Item listing fields (Still Looking / Available, price, priority) exist only for `want`/`sell` collections, whose artisan collection page marks each card like the Wishlist Image (`ArtisanListingMarks` in the card's `badges` slot: the _Found_ or _Sold_ stamp on a greyed card, _Top Priority_ with the card `highlight`ed on wishlists, and the _Offer_ or _Ask_ price). A collection page opens with a `UPageHeader` (`detailPageHeaderUi(false)`: the name, the message as its description, and the actions in `#links`) under a navbar breadcrumb ending at _My Collection_. Its actions are `CollectionHeaderActions` (`app/components/collection/HeaderActions.vue`), in the detail-page order: the labeled primary action in its default slot (_Share_ on artisan collections), then Edit and Delete as icon-only `SharedRecordActions` (no `@history`, so no History; Edit opens the collection form, Delete goes through `useConfirmDelete` and the component emits `deleted`, which pages bind to `onDeleted` from `useCollection` to drop the collection from the user store and go back). Artisan collections sort by _Maker & Sculpt_ or _Colorway Name_ (`sort_by`); there is no manual sort page any more: the item `order` is set by dragging in the Wishlist Image, and an artisan collection still holding the old `order|asc` falls back to the default on its page and in the form through `artisanCollectionSort` (`app/utils/collections.ts`, beside `artisanCollectionSortItems`, the form's choices). Keyset and keyboard collections keep `order|asc`. Collection pages show those actions and item actions only to the owner (`isOwner` from `useCollection`), since shared collections are public. `useCollection` loads the collection with `useRequestFetch()`, not `$fetch`: collection pages render on the server (published ones are shared), and only the forwarded cookies let RLS show the owner a private collection on a direct load. The form prefills the Discord username from `userStore.social.discord` (also when the profile loads after the form opens). `POST /api/users/:id/collections` and `/collections/:collection` are session-owner-only; inserts take `uid` from the session and updates return 404 when RLS changed no row. RLS (`20261012000000_collection_policies`): owners manage their collections and items (items only inside their own collections); anyone reads published collections and their items, and private items stay owner-only.
- **Save to Collection**: `app/components/shared/SaveToCollection.vue`, the Save/Move menu used across modules. In save mode it always renders for signed-in users and ends with a New Collection item that opens `CollectionModalCollectionForm` with the item's category preset and `lock-category`; in move mode it hides when no other collection of the category exists. It has two roots (menu and modal), so it sets `inheritAttrs: false` and binds `$attrs` to the menu.
