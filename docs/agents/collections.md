# Collections

Feature reference for Keebdex agents; project-wide rules live in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

User collections, the Save/Move menu every module uses, and the Trading Hub.

- **Collections** (`app/pages/collection/`, `app/components/collection/modal/`): `user_collections.intent` is shown to users as **My Collection** (`keep`), **Wishlist** (`want`), and **For Sale or Trade** (`sell`) through `collectionIntentItems` (`app/utils/collections.ts`); never show the word "intent". `CollectionForm.vue` validates with `collectionSchema` (`app/utils/schemas/collection.ts`, Discord contact required unless `keep`) and never sends `published`, which only the Share modal changes. A published `want`/`sell` artisan collection is listed in the Trading Hub (`/artisan/marketplace`, `GET /api/marketplace?page=&size=&intent=`), and the Share modal says so. The endpoint pages collections with at least one listed (`exchange`) item, newest first (`size` default 20, capped at 50; an inner join on the items keeps empty collections out of pages and the total; `intent=want|sell` narrows to one kind), and returns `{ data, total }` with each collection's listed `items` (colorway with sculpt and maker names). The page keeps `intent` (All / Buying / Selling toolbar tabs, switching resets the page) and `page` (`usePagination(20)`) in the URL. `ArtisanMarketplaceListing` titles each listing with the collection name plus a Buying/Selling badge, Discord contact, cap count, and date, fits the avatar row to the breakpoint, and shows skeletons while loading and `UEmpty` when empty; its slideover shows offer/asking prices and Top Priority badges, links each cap to its sculpt page (`?cid=`), and offers View Collection and Copy Discord (only when a contact is set). Item listing fields (Still Looking / Available, price, priority) and the found/sold overlay exist only for `want`/`sell` collections. Collection pages show Share, Manual Sort, Edit, Delete, and item actions only to the owner (`isOwner` from `useCollection`), since shared collections are public. The form prefills the Discord username from `userStore.social.discord` (also when the profile loads after the form opens). `POST /api/users/:id/collections` and `/collections/:collection` are session-owner-only; inserts take `uid` from the session and updates return 404 when RLS changed no row. RLS (`20261012000000_collection_policies`): owners manage their collections and items (items only inside their own collections); anyone reads published collections and their items, and private items stay owner-only.
- **Save to Collection**: `app/components/shared/SaveToCollection.vue`, the Save/Move menu used across modules. In save mode it always renders for signed-in users and ends with a New Collection item that opens `CollectionModalCollectionForm` with the item's category preset and `lock-category`; in move mode it hides when no other collection of the category exists. It has two roots (menu and modal), so it sets `inheritAttrs: false` and binds `$attrs` to the menu.
