# Keebdex

**Keebdex** is a purpose-built platform for keyboard collectors to organize, sync, and share the full keyboard trinity: keyboards, keysets, and artisans.

**Visit the live site: [keebdex.org](https://keebdex.org)**

## Key Features

- **Unified Trinity Database**: Keyboards, keysets, and artisans live together in one managed database, moving beyond the artisan-only Google Docs into detailed pages you can browse, search, and collect.
- **Beautiful, Intuitive Design**: A sleek, modern interface built for collectors, with clear layouts that work on any screen and images sized for your device so every page stays fast, even on phones.
- **Make It Yours**: Pick from a growing set of theme presets, each with its own colors, fonts, and component style, in light, dark, or system mode. Your choice is saved to your account and follows you to every device.
- **Secure Login, Synced Everywhere**: Sign in instantly with your Google or Discord account. Your collections, theme, and color mode follow you, so everything looks the same on every device you use.
- **Powerful Collection Tools**: Create unlimited collections across keyboards, keysets, and artisans and save new finds from any page. Artisan collections can be a shelf of caps you own, a wishlist, or caps for sale or trade, with prices and a custom order.
- **Search Everything**: Find any keyboard, keyset, maker, sculpt, or colorway from one search box that updates as you type, with results grouped by category so you can jump straight to its page.
- **Community Submissions**: Propose colorways, kits, and keyboard variants, or a whole new sculpt, keyset, or keyboard, through a guided step-by-step wizard, reviewed by moderators before going live.
- **Share Your Passion**: Share artisan collections by link, turn wishlists and colorway or keyboard cards into eye-catching images for any community, and meet buyers and sellers in the Trading Hub.

See [Why Choose Keebdex?](https://keebdex.org/why-choose-keebdex) for a comparison with other collector tools.
